Privacy Policy

How the CFPMI school-management app collects, uses, protects, and deletes your information.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CFPMI ("we", "us", the "app") handles personal information for the people who use it — students, parents/guardians, teachers, and school administrators. The app is a school-management platform used by the institution to run its daily academic and administrative operations.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, role (student / teacher / admin / parent), the school you belong to, and sign-in credentials.
  • School records you enter or that are entered about you — class/level, subjects, timetable, marks and results, attendance, homework, fee records (charges and recorded payments), announcements, and club/group membership.
  • Content you create — notes, posts, messages, uploaded materials, and quiz/learning activity.
  • Technical data — basic device and app information and crash/diagnostic reports used to keep the app stable.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the core school operations: enrolment, timetables, marks, attendance, fees records, announcements, classroom lessons, and learning materials.
  • To authenticate you and apply the correct permissions for your role.
  • To keep your data in sync and available offline where supported.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

3. How your information is stored and protected

Data is stored on managed cloud infrastructure (Supabase/PostgreSQL and Firebase). Access is controlled by server-side security rules so that each school's data is isolated and each person only sees what their role permits. Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

We share information only: (a) within your own school, according to role (e.g. a teacher sees their class; a student sees their own records); (b) with the infrastructure providers above strictly to operate the service; or (c) where required by law. We do not share your data with advertisers.

5. Children & students

CFPMI is operated for and through an educational institution. Where students are minors, the school acts as the party responsible for obtaining any parental/guardian consent required under local law, and uses the app to carry out its legitimate educational functions. Parents/guardians may contact the school to review a student's information.

6. Data retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the school's services and meet legal/record-keeping obligations. When an account or its data is deleted (see below), it is removed from active systems within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.

7. Your rights & account/data deletion

Request deletion of your account and data

You can request deletion of your CFPMI account and the personal data associated with it at any time:

  • In the app: go to Profile → Settings → Request account deletion, or
  • By email: send a request from your account's email address to privacy@cfpmi.cm with the subject "Account deletion request".

What gets deleted: your profile and sign-in credentials, your private notes and content, your messages, and the personal records tied to your account.

What may be retained: records the school is legally required to keep (for example official academic results or financial records), and anonymised/aggregated data that no longer identifies you. These are retained only as long as required by law or the institution's obligations.

Timeline: deletion requests are actioned and removed from active systems within 30 days. You will receive confirmation once completed.

A dedicated deletion page is also available at delete-account.html.

You may also request access to, or correction of, your personal information using the same contact.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@cfpmi.cm.

Note to the operator: replace privacy@cfpmi.cm with a monitored contact address before publishing to app stores.
