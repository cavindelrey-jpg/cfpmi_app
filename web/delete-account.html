Delete your CFPMI account

This page explains how to request deletion of your CFPMI account and the personal data associated with it.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open the CFPMI app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Request account deletion.
  3. Confirm the request.

Option 2 — By email

Send a request from the email address on your account to:

Email a deletion request

Address: agencydylansdigital@gmail.com — subject "Account deletion request".

What is deleted

Deleted: your profile and sign-in credentials, private notes and content, messages, and the personal records tied to your account.

Retained (only where legally required): official records the institution must keep, such as academic results or financial records, and anonymised data that no longer identifies you.

Timeline: removed from active systems within 30 days; you’ll get a confirmation.
