1.Introduction

Green Hills Academia ("Green Hills Academia", "we", "us", or "our") provides an educational platform that schools use to support teaching, learning, and day-to-day school administration. The platform is used by students, teachers, and school administrators.

We take the privacy of our users seriously, particularly because the platform is used by schools and young learners. This policy describes what information the application handles, why it is needed, how it is protected, and the choices and rights available to users. By using Green Hills Academia, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2.Information We Collect

We only collect information that the platform needs in order to function as an educational tool. We distinguish between two broad categories:

Information you actively provide

This is information entered by you, your teachers, or your school when creating accounts, setting up classes, or using features of the platform — for example, names, account details, academic content, and messages.

Information generated automatically

This is technical information that the application or its underlying services may generate automatically when the app is used — for example, basic device and app information needed to keep the service running, secure, and reliable.

The sections below describe each category in more detail. We do not collect information that the application does not actually need to operate.

3.How We Use Information

We use information solely to provide and improve the educational service, including to:

  • Create and manage student, teacher, and administrator accounts.
  • Authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • Display classes, subjects, timetables, assignments, and academic content.
  • Deliver announcements, school events, and school communication features.
  • Enable messaging and collaboration between appropriate members of a school.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability and performance of the app.
  • Protect the platform against misuse, unauthorised access, and security threats.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use student information for advertising or for any purpose unrelated to education and the operation of the platform.

4.Account and Profile Information

To use the platform, users have an account. Account and profile information may include:

  • A name or display name.
  • An account identifier and authentication details (such as a username, email, or sign-in credential) used to sign in securely.
  • A role within a school (for example, student, teacher, or administrator).
  • Optional profile details that a user or school chooses to add.

Authentication credentials are used only to verify identity and secure access. Where sign-in is handled through a third-party authentication service, that service processes credentials on our behalf, as described in the Data Sharing section.

5.Student, Teacher, and School Information

Because Green Hills Academia is used by schools, the platform may handle information relating to school activities, such as:

  • Student academic information (for example, assignments and related academic content).
  • Classes and subjects a user is associated with.
  • Timetables and school events.
  • Announcements and other school communications.
  • Messages exchanged through school communication features.

Much of this information is created and managed by schools and their staff. In many cases, a school acts as the owner of this information and determines how it is used within the platform. Where a school administers accounts on behalf of its students and staff, the school is responsible for obtaining any necessary permissions and for managing access in line with its own policies.

6.App Usage and Technical Information

When the application is used, limited technical information may be generated automatically to keep the service working correctly and securely. This may include:

  • Basic device and application information (such as app version and general device type).
  • Information needed to deliver notifications to a device, where this feature is used.
  • Diagnostic or error information that helps us identify and fix problems.

This technical information is used to operate, secure, and improve the platform. It is not used to build advertising profiles. The specific technical data available depends on the features in use and the services that support the app.

7.How We Store and Protect Information

Information is stored using reputable cloud infrastructure and database services. We rely on a combination of technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, such as:

  • Encryption of data in transit between the app and its services.
  • Access controls that limit who can view or manage information based on their role.
  • Authentication measures to protect accounts.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We work to protect information using industry-standard practices and to respond appropriately if a security issue arises.

8.Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal information. We share information only where necessary to operate the platform, including with service providers that support core functionality. These providers may include, as applicable:

  • Authentication services used to sign users in securely.
  • Database hosting and cloud storage services used to store platform data.
  • Notification services used to deliver messages or alerts to devices.
  • Diagnostic or analytics services used to monitor reliability and improve the app.

These providers process information on our behalf and only to the extent needed to deliver their service. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the platform.

9.Data Retention

We retain information for as long as it is needed to provide the service and to fulfil the purposes described in this policy. Retention periods may depend on the type of information and on a school's own requirements.

When information is no longer required — for example, when an account or a school's use of the platform ends — it may be deleted or anonymised, subject to any legal or administrative obligations and to the instructions of the school that owns the information.

10.Children's and Students' Privacy

Green Hills Academia is designed for use in an educational setting and may be used by students, including minors. We handle student information carefully and only for educational purposes.

Student accounts are typically created and managed by a school. Where required, the school is responsible for obtaining appropriate consent from parents or guardians and for managing student access in accordance with applicable laws and its own policies. We do not knowingly use student information for advertising or for any purpose unrelated to education.

If you believe a student's information has been provided or used in a way that is not appropriate, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

11.User Rights and Data Requests

Depending on your role and applicable law, you may be able to:

  • Access the information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled.

Because schools often manage accounts and information on behalf of their students and staff, some requests may be directed to, or handled through, the relevant school. To make a request, please contact us or your school administrator using the details in the Contact section. We will respond in line with applicable requirements.
